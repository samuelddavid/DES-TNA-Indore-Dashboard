Key Findings from the Pilot — प्रमुख निष्कर्ष

  • Staff capability clusters at "basic awareness to working knowledge" — the need is for practical strengthening, not remedial re-education.
  • Three themes qualify as immediate workshop priorities: Survey Operations & Field Data Quality, Data Management & Scrutiny, and Digital Tools & Office Systems.
  • 91.3% of respondents have ≤2 hours per week available for learning — training must be compact, modular, and blended.
  • 30.4% rely solely on a smartphone — all materials must be mobile-compatible.
  • STATA/E-Views (91.3%), R/Python (82.6%), and AI Tools (78.3%) attract overwhelmingly beginner-level demand — advanced analytics training should be sequenced after foundational digital modules.
  • Workload and lack of time is the single dominant barrier, cited by 73.9% of respondents in all its combinations.

W1
Survey Operations, Interview Practice and Field Data Quality
Phase 1A — Start First
Primary audience: Block Level Investigators
Participation: Assistant Statistical Officers
Duration: 1.5 days + short follow-up
Mode: Blended — strong classroom component
Priority: Very High
  • Standardise field protocols and statistical schedule handling
  • Improve interview quality and respondent interaction techniques
  • Reduce avoidable field errors and omissions
  • Build routine field-level quality checks into daily work practice
W2
Data Management, Scrutiny and Quality Assurance
Phase 1A — Immediately After W1
Primary audience: Block Level Investigators and Assistant Statistical Officers
Duration: 1 day + practice sheets
Mode: Blended
Priority: Very High
  • Strengthen scrutiny of completed schedules and data entries
  • Identify common error types and inconsistency patterns
  • Improve record keeping discipline and validation routines
  • Introduce simple quality assurance checklists for field use
W3
Digital Tools, e-Office and Office Workflow Management
Phase 1A / Early 1B
Primary audience: Block Level Investigators and Assistant Statistical Officers
Selective participation: District Statistical Officers
Duration: 1 day
Mode: Blended — demonstration-heavy
Priority: Very High
  • Improve practical use of Microsoft Office and official templates
  • Strengthen e-Office and File/DAK workflow management
  • Improve digital communication and document handling
  • Reduce process delays caused by weak digital fluency
W4
Reporting, File and Record Systems, and Statistical Communication
Phase 1B
Primary audience: Block Level Investigators and Assistant Statistical Officers
Selective participation: District Statistical Officers
Duration: 0.5–1 day
Mode: Classroom-led with templates and examples
Priority: High
  • Improve noting, drafting, and routine statistical communication
  • Standardise file movement and record maintenance procedures
  • Improve preparation of concise reports, briefs, and statistical returns
  • Connect documentation quality with upstream data quality outcomes
W5
Applied Data Analysis, Visualisation and Statistical Software
Phase 1B — Targeted Track
Primary audience: Assistant Statistical Officers
Selective participation: District Statistical Officers
Duration: 2 days or 4 half-day sessions
Mode: Blended — guided hands-on practice
Priority: High (Targeted)
  • Build confidence in descriptive analysis and simple data interpretation
  • Improve preparation of charts, tables, and short analytical notes
  • Introduce beginner-level statistical software workflow (STATA / R)
  • Connect statistical concepts to operational outputs and planning reports
